SANTA CLAUS

Santa Claus is a legendary figure known for bringing joy and gifts to children around the world.

He is known by different names in different countries and is often associated with the North Pole.

Stories say he travels around the world on a very special night each year.

⭐ FUN FACT ⭐

The modern image of Santa Claus was influenced by traditions surrounding Saint Nicholas, a fourth-century bishop known for his generosity.
